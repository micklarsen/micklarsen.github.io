Welcome to my corner of the internet.

This is my personal site - a place to write about things I find interesting, document projects I'm working on, and share thoughts that don't fit anywhere else. No particular theme, no schedule, just whatever's on my mind.

If something here resonates with you, feel free to reach out by mail.
